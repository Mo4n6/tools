## What you may do without asking

- State accurately that your work is based on, forked from, or compatible with `Mo4n6 Tools`.
- Refer to the project or a tool by name in documentation, articles, reviews, comparisons, and talks.
- Keep the attribution and `Required Notice:` lines that the licence already requires you to keep.

## What needs written permission

- Naming a fork, distribution, hosted service, or product after `Mo4n6 Tools` or any tool name, or using a name confusingly similar to one.
- Using the names in a way that implies endorsement, affiliation, official status, or that your build is the canonical one.
- Using the project logo or wordmark as the identity of your own project or service.

If you fork and modify, give your fork its own name. Describe the relationship in prose — "a fork of `Mo4n6 Tools`" is fine and accurate; naming the fork `Mo4n6 Tools Plus` is not.

## Why this file exists

These are security tools. A modified build circulating under this name would inherit trust it has not earned, and users have no practical way to tell the difference. The name is the only part of this project that meaningfully signals provenance, which is precisely why it is reserved.
